What are you building and how is it different than what is already out there? *
Solution and unique value proposition. If relevant, please list and describe any IP that is associated with your company

Firmic is the Operating System for AI-Native Companies. We provide AI powered operational infrastructure that enables businesses to launch, operate, and scale from a single platform.

Traditional providers such as Regus and Servcorp offer office infrastructure. Firmic goes beyond this by combining virtual offices, communications, compliance, document management, reporting, software integrations, and an AI workforce into one unified operating system.

Through Firmic, a company can create a workspace, rent a virtual office, manage documents and tasks, monitor compliance, generate reports, connect business software, and deploy AI employees from day one.

Our long term vision is to build a coordinated AI workforce managed through Sonny, our AI operations orchestrator, and Hermes, our compliance and governance engine. These systems will coordinate specialized AI agents across operations, sales, support, finance, legal, compliance, and administration.

Our intellectual property is centered around the Firmic platform architecture, including the AI workforce orchestration layer, compliance intelligence workflows, business infrastructure management system, and the integration framework that connects virtual office services with AI driven business operations.



What makes your team special and how are you best positioned to solve this problem?
You can share your team's background, and inspiration behind your startup
Firmic was born from a simple observation: starting and operating a company remains fragmented, expensive, and operationally complex. Entrepreneurs often spend significant time coordinating office providers, compliance requirements, communications, software tools, and administrative workflows instead of focusing on growth.

As the founder, I have spent years building and studying technology, startups, business infrastructure, and AI driven systems. Through that experience, I identified an opportunity to rethink how modern companies are launched and operated. Rather than providing another isolated software tool, Firmic aims to become the operating system that brings together business infrastructure, compliance, communications, software, and AI workforce management into a single platform.

What positions us uniquely is that we are not approaching the problem from a traditional office services perspective or a standalone software perspective. We are combining both. Traditional providers offer infrastructure, while software providers offer individual tools. Firmic connects these worlds through AI powered operational infrastructure.

We have already built a functioning MVP that includes company workspaces, virtual offices, document management, task management, reporting, compliance monitoring through Hermes, and operational coordination through Sonny AI. This hands on execution and deep focus on the problem gives us a strong foundation to continue building the future of AI native business infrastructure.

What is the 'Why Now' for your business? *
Why is this the right time for you to solve this problem using this solution, and why is this a good time for you to be in Abu Dhabi and the MENA region
The timing for Firmic is driven by the convergence of three major trends. First, AI systems are rapidly evolving from productivity tools into capable digital workers that can perform operational tasks across sales, support, compliance, administration, and business operations. Second, businesses are increasingly remote-first and digital-first, creating demand for infrastructure that can be accessed and managed from anywhere. Third, company formation, compliance, communications, and business services are becoming increasingly digitized.

Despite these shifts, entrepreneurs still rely on fragmented providers for virtual offices, compliance, software, communications, and operations. We believe the next generation of companies will launch with AI employees from day one and will require a unified operating system rather than a collection of disconnected services.

Abu Dhabi and the wider MENA region are uniquely positioned for this transformation. Governments across the region are actively investing in AI, digital transformation, entrepreneurship, and business friendly regulatory environments. Hub71 and Abu Dhabi have established themselves as global leaders in supporting AI driven innovation and technology startups.

Firmic aligns closely with this vision by enabling entrepreneurs to launch, operate, and scale companies through a single platform. As we expand, we also see significant opportunities to integrate with regulated company formation providers, compliance services, and business infrastructure partners across the UAE and the broader MENA region, making Abu Dhabi an ideal base for growth.
